## German Language 
Core sounds:
- ie -- pronounced as 'ee' e.g sieben
- ei -- pronounced as 'eye' eg zeit
- äu and eu -- pronounced as 'oy' e.g Häuser(houses), neu
- z -- pronounced 'ts'e.g zeit (tsyte)
- w -- pronounced 'v' -- e.g wasser
- v -- pronounced 'f' e.g vater
- j -- pronounced 'y' e.g jahren
- ä ≈ "eh" (like "bed")
- ö -- say 'eh with your lips rounded like you are saying' oh'
- ü ≈ say "ee" while rounding your lips
- St → German pronounces initial "st" as "sht"
